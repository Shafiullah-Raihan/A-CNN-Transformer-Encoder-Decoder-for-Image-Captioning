## 📌 Overview

This repository presents the research manuscript **"A CNN-Transformer Encoder-Decoder for Image Captioning: Label Smoothing, Backbone Fine-Tuning, and Length-Normalized Decoding."**

The research investigates a hybrid deep learning approach to automatic image caption generation, combining Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) for visual feature extraction with a Transformer-based encoder-decoder architecture for natural language generation.

The study focuses on three important components of image captioning systems:

- Label smoothing for improved training and generalisation.
- CNN backbone fine-tuning for visual feature representation.
- Length-normalised decoding for caption generation.

The research contributes to the study of vision-language modelling by exploring techniques for generating meaningful natural language descriptions from visual information.

## 🎯 Research Objectives

The primary objectives of this research are:

1. To investigate a hybrid CNN-Transformer architecture for automatic image caption generation.
2. To explore the role of CNN-based visual feature extraction in image understanding.
3. To examine Transformer-based encoder-decoder modelling for caption generation.
4. To investigate label smoothing as a training regularisation technique.
5. To study CNN backbone fine-tuning for visual representation learning.
6. To explore length-normalised decoding in the generation of image descriptions.

## 🧠 Proposed Architecture

The research focuses on a CNN-Transformer encoder-decoder framework.

### 1. CNN-Based Visual Feature Extraction

A Convolutional Neural Network processes the input image and extracts visual representations that can be used by the caption generation model.

### 2. Transformer Encoder-Decoder

The Transformer architecture supports the modelling of relationships between visual information and natural language sequences.

### 3. Label Smoothing

Label smoothing is investigated as a regularisation technique to reduce excessive confidence during model training.

### 4. Backbone Fine-Tuning

Fine-tuning allows the CNN backbone to adapt its visual representations to the image captioning task.

### 5. Length-Normalised Decoding

Length-normalised decoding is explored to account for sequence length when selecting generated captions.

## 🌍 Research Significance

Automatic image captioning is an important research area at the intersection of computer vision and natural language processing.

Generating accurate and meaningful descriptions of visual content requires both effective image representation and language generation capabilities.

Hybrid CNN-Transformer architectures offer an approach to combining these capabilities.

This research explores training and decoding strategies relevant to image caption generation and contributes to ongoing investigation into multimodal artificial intelligence.

Potential applications of image captioning research include:

- Assistive technologies for visually impaired individuals.
- Automatic image description and accessibility.
- Intelligent image retrieval.
- Visual content understanding.
- Multimedia indexing.
- Human-computer interaction.
